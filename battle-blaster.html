Overview / 01

Built for reusable combat.

Battle Blaster puts the player in direct control of a tank navigating an arena of hostile towers.

I developed the project with C++ and Blueprints, using a shared pawn combat architecture to keep turret rotation, projectile spawning, and damage handling consistent across the player tank and enemy tower variants.

The movement and targeting systems emphasize readable, predictable behavior: collision-safe traversal for the tank, smooth constant-rate tracking for towers, and timer-gated projectile firing.

Engineering / 02

Core systems

The implementation separates shared combat responsibilities from pawn-specific movement and behavior, making the project easier to extend without duplicating core logic.

01

Shared pawn architecture

Extracted turret rotation, projectile spawning, and damage handling into reusable logic shared by tank and tower variants.

02

Swept tank movement

Converted throttle and steering input into swept translation and rotation against a box-based collision root to prevent tunneling and invalid overlaps.

03

Turret targeting

Calculated target direction and interpolated turret rotation at a constant rate, producing smooth and predictable enemy tracking.

04

Firing cadence

Gated projectile spawning with timers so enemy attacks remain consistent, readable, and easy to tune.
