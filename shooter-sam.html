Overview / 01

Enemies that investigate.

Shooter Sam is a tactical third-person shooter where enemy squads react to what they can see and what they can hear.

I developed layered Behavior Tree states for patrol, investigation, pursuit, and attack. Gunfire becomes shared last-known position and direction data, letting nearby AI coordinate an investigation without giving them impossible knowledge through walls.

The level structure is driven by an event-based objective framework, while reusable components and targeted runtime checks keep health UI and AI updates efficient.

Engineering / 02

Core systems

The game connects perception, coordination, objectives, animation, health, and optimization through focused systems that communicate through events and shared state.

01

Tactical behavior tree

Used services, decorators, and visibility traces for clean transitions between patrol, investigation, pursuit, and attack without tracking the player through walls.

02

Squad awareness

Converted gunfire into shared last-known position and direction data so nearby AI coordinate an investigation before returning to individual decisions.

03

Event-driven objectives

Collection, elimination, and key events update GameMode state and unlock the exit only after every requirement is satisfied.

04

Animation state

Combined player input, velocity, movement direction, and actor rotation for responsive transitions among locomotion, aiming, and attack states.

05

Reusable health component

Broadcast normalized health through a multicast delegate, allowing player and AI UMG health bars to update without Tick polling.

06

AI workload control

Used octree queries, visibility traces, and NavMesh connectivity checks to suspend Tick for unreachable enemies and reduce unnecessary processing.
